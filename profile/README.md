We are the Environmental Contaminants group at the Department of Environmental Monitoring and Research, [Swedish Museum of Natural History](https://www.nrm.se/engelska/in-english/environmental-monitoring/environmental-contaminant-monitoring) (NRM), and this is where we share the code and data packages we work with. The group runs the Swedish national monitoring of contaminants in marine and freshwater biota, with time series going back to the 1960s, using samples from the Environmental Specimen Bank. Here you will find, among other things, [fmcom](https://github.com/NRM-MOC/fmcom), a dataset of contaminant concentrations in Swedish marine and freshwater biota, and [fmcom-annual-report](https://github.com/NRM-MOC/fmcom-annual-report), the pipeline that produces the yearly monitoring reports.
